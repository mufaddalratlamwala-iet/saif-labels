“A quality-conscious team, testing every roll before it leaves the plant.”

Our workforce is trained to hold labels to international quality standards, not just print them fast. Every batch runs through the same latest-generation machines and the same trained eyes before it reaches a client, whether the order is a standard catalogue item or a specification built around one customer's product.

Clients come back to us for the finishing: consistent gloss and colour, dependable adhesion, and — where the application calls for it — tamper-evident and special-effect finishes that hold up in the field. We keep both standardised and fully customised ranges running side by side, so a small pharmacy order and a large FMCG contract get the same attention on the press.

How a label gets made

Five stages, all under one roof

Design and plate work happen before the reel touches a press; inspection happens both during printing and again after slitting.

01
Aa

Plate mounting & design

Artwork is finalised and plates are mounted to the client's exact size and colour specification.

02

9-colour UV printing

Our Neel Peter press lays down up to nine colours in a single pass, cured under UV for sharp, durable print.

03

In-line inspection

Every reel passes an inspection machine that flags print defects before the roll is cut to size.

04

Slitting

Master rolls are slit to the exact web width each client's applicator machine requires.

05

Grafotronic re-inspection

A final rewind and inspection pass on the Grafotronic line before rolls are packed for dispatch.
